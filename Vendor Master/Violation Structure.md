# Violation Structures

## Minor Violations 

**Explanation:** *These violations are lesser we allow 3-4 of these before considering termination from the platform*

- Selling a stone and not updating inventory/only telling us when we ask for it for inquiry
- Accidentally Sending the wrong stone but coordinating a correction in a timely fashion (2 times)
  - If correction takes too long and the Sale falls through, instant minor violation
- Altered the stone (heated, recut or otherwise treated) after uploading and neglected to update until we inquire on the stone for a Memo.

## Major Violations

**Explanation:** *These violations are very serious and will warrant termination from the platform after another minor violation*

- Selling a stone and not updating inventory/only telling us when we ask for it for a sale
- Misrepresenting the availability of an item (example: a Vendor claims a stone is available when contacted and then either tries to give us a completely different stone or then changes what they say later)
- Sending the wrong stone on purpose 
- Changing item pricing without updating their inventory until the item is inquired about and **not** honoring original pricing.
- Vendor does not respond to a sales inquiry within an acceptable amount of time.

## Immediate Termination

**Explanation:** *These are above violations, these elicit immediate termination from the platform and could be considered tantamount to fraud* 

- Attempting to pass off simulants as real stones.
- Selling items that are found to be stolen.
- We cannot contact the vendor for over a month despite continued correspondence
