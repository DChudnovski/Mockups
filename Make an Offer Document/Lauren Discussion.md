# Ideas from Lauren Discussion

***Serving Make an Offers***

- Make-An-Offers should be for certain types of items not just open to everything. Eligibility could be determined by Activity Analytics (if they go for a long time without any views; viewed alot but never bought etc.)
- If a customer wants to do MAO they should have to enter details like email etc.
- After MAO is agreed to we should have a lock-in action that is limited to finializing purchase within 20 mins of finishing Negotiation.
- Only Vendors in good standing should be able to create MAOs.
- For higher priced items (20k and higher) there should be a threshold for offers to be sent to Salespeople so that they can review and look into finalizing offers directly.

For Promotions we should hold and consider direct implications before directly pushing things to NXC site, as of right now there are considerations for pricing and shifts that could lead to negative associations that we'd like to avoid for the time being. Having the infrastructure built and ready is a good idea but we do not need it to be pushed out yet.
