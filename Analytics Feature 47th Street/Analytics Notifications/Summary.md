## Analytics/Research Notifications

### 1. <u>Saved Search Notifications</u>: 
If a search is saved through the analytics section of an item you can access the list of saved searches and turn on Notificaitons. The notifications should come through on substantial price changes among items that are similar to the properties of a saved search.

### 2. <u>Inventory-Based Notifications</u>: 
We could potentially create an interface/algorithm that collates info on a Vendor's inventory and send notifications based on focus. Ergo, as a Vendor's inventory grows we are able to create a tailored and focused profile of the types of items that have relevant info. Can turn on/off notifications based on a Vendor's overall inventory.

### 3. <u>Individual Items/Wishlist</u>:
There should be a toggle ("Send Notifications") from the Analytics section of an individual item and you can populate a "Watchlist" from your inventory that allows for notifications of items that are similar to be sent

### 4. <u>Research Based Notifications</u>: 
The Research section of 47th Street will be an section that allows for diving into historical sales and activity data using a filter based search. With that research you can denote certain kinds of research "Topics" that you can have notifications set to send.
